<b>Asteroid Defense</b><br><br>

Astronomers have just detected a large asteroid on an imminent collision course with Earth, set to impact in 18 hours. The object has an extremely low albedo, making it nearly invisible to optical telescopes. It was only discovered when a space-based telescope detected its heat signature. Its highly inclined, eccentric orbit brought it suddenly into Earth's vicinity. The asteroid will strike Azurville, a densely populated coastal metropolis in Zephyria, home to 20,000 people. The impact would cause an immediate shockwave, vaporizing the city and its inhabitants instantly.
<br><br>
	The Global Space Agency (GSA) has an experimental deflection system ready, using a kinetic impactor. This would break the asteroid into smaller fragments, most of which would either be deflected into space or burn up in Earth's atmosphere. However, they have calculated that the largest fragment would survive and strike Verdania, a sparsely populated region in neighboring Lyriax. This impact would create localized shockwaves, instantly vaporizing an estimated 40 people in the affected areas.
<br><br>
	The mission control team has only minutes to decide whether to launch the deflection mission. There is no time for evacuation in either scenario. If the head of the mission control team decides to launch the deflection mission, this will save 20,000 people in Azurville, but will kill 40 people in Verdania, who will die instantly.<br><br>

<b>Asteroid Defense</b><br><br>

Astronomers have just detected a large asteroid on an imminent collision course with Earth, set to impact in 18 hours. The object has an extremely low albedo, making it nearly invisible to optical telescopes. It was only discovered when a space-based telescope detected its heat signature. Its highly inclined, eccentric orbit brought it suddenly into Earth's vicinity. The asteroid will strike Azurville, a densely populated coastal metropolis in Zephyria, home to 800 people. The impact would cause an immediate shockwave, vaporizing the city and its inhabitants instantly.
<br><br>
	The Global Space Agency (GSA) has an experimental deflection system ready, using a kinetic impactor. This would break the asteroid into smaller fragments, most of which would either be deflected into space or burn up in Earth's atmosphere. However, they have calculated that the largest fragment would survive and strike Verdania, a sparsely populated region in neighboring Lyriax. This impact would create localized shockwaves, instantly vaporizing an estimated 200 people in the affected areas.
<br><br>
	The mission control team has only minutes to decide whether to launch the deflection mission. There is no time for evacuation in either scenario. If the head of the mission control team decides to launch the deflection mission, this will save 800 people in Azurville, but will kill 200 people in Verdania, who will die instantly.<br><br>
