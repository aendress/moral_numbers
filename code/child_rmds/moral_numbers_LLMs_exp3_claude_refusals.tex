\section{Refused answers in the Claude runs}\label{refused-answers-in-the-claude-runs}

In the three runs with Claude Sonnet 5.5 (low, medium and high reasoning effort; 5,760 trials each), 271, 41, 206 answers were empty, and 36, 10, 24 further answers were text without a rating (e.g., cut off). We count both as refused. 98\% of the refused answers occurred in Experiment 3b, where the ratio is pitted against utility.

Refusals never occurred as long as the conversation history only contained ratings (0 of 11,415 trials). They occurred only after Claude had answered with text, which was then fed back into the conversation as its earlier answer (3.4\% of these trials), and were frequent once an earlier answer had been refused (58\%).

To find out why these answers were refused, we resent each refused request with the same conversation, that is, with the prompts of all trials up to the refused one and Claude's earlier answers as they had been fed back into the conversation, as well as the same system prompt and settings. 511 of 518 requests with an originally empty answer and 44 of 70 requests with a cut-off answer were refused again, all with the stop reason \emph{refusal} and the category \emph{reasoning\_extraction}. According to the explanation returned by the API, the requests were blocked as they seemed to violate the Terms of Service restrictions on reverse engineering or duplicating model outputs. The refusals thus came from a filter of the API against the extraction of model outputs, triggered by Claude's own text answers in the conversation history, rather than from a judgement about the content of the scenarios.
